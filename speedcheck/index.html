How SpeedCheck Works

SpeedCheck uses Cloudflare's official speed test engine to measure your real internet connection performance. Your browser communicates directly with the nearest Cloudflare edge datacenter — part of a network spanning 330+ cities worldwide — using multiple parallel HTTPS requests with dynamic payload sizes.

Unlike many speed tests that route through a single server, SpeedCheck connects to Cloudflare's anycast edge, eliminating ISP artificial caching and revealing your true real-world throughput. The test runs three phases automatically: latency measurement, download bandwidth testing, and upload bandwidth testing.

Understanding Your Results

⬇️ Download Speed

How fast data transfers from the internet to your device, measured in Mbps (megabits per second). High download speed is critical for streaming 4K video (25+ Mbps), fast webpage loads, and quick software downloads.

⬆️ Upload Speed

How fast your device sends data to the internet. Essential for crisp Zoom and Teams video calls (5–10 Mbps), Twitch streaming, uploading cloud backups, and sending large email attachments.

⚡ Latency (Ping)

The round-trip reaction time in milliseconds for a data packet to reach the edge server and return. Lower ping (under 30 ms) is vital for lag-free multiplayer gaming and snappy web browsing.

🔄 Loaded Latency

Ping measured while your connection is actively downloading or uploading heavy traffic. If this is significantly higher than your unloaded ping, your router has bufferbloat — meaning video calls and gaming lag whenever someone else in the house streams or downloads.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does SpeedCheck work?

SpeedCheck uses Cloudflare's official speed test engine. Your browser communicates directly with Cloudflare's nearest edge datacenter (330+ cities worldwide) using multiple parallel HTTPS requests. It measures download bandwidth, upload bandwidth, unloaded latency (ping), and loaded latency (bufferbloat) — all without ISP caching interference.

Why does SpeedCheck auto-start without clicking a button?

SpeedCheck is designed for instant results — like fast.com by Netflix. The speed test begins automatically when you open the page so you get your download speed in seconds without needing to click anything. You can restart the test anytime with the green refresh button.

What is the difference between unloaded and loaded latency?

Unloaded latency (ping) measures your connection's reaction time when idle. Loaded latency measures ping while actively downloading or uploading heavy data. If loaded latency is much higher than unloaded, your router has bufferbloat — video calls and gaming will lag when someone else on your network is streaming or downloading.

What is a good internet speed?

For general browsing, 5–10 Mbps download is sufficient. HD streaming needs 10–15 Mbps, 4K streaming needs 25+ Mbps per screen. Video conferencing requires 5–10 Mbps upload with low jitter. Competitive gaming needs under 30 ms ping. Heavy cloud backups benefit from 100+ Mbps.

How is SpeedCheck different from the Advanced Speed Test?

SpeedCheck (this page) is the minimalist, instant-start version focused purely on your download speed with optional details. The Advanced Speed Test shows a full gauge visualization, detailed metric cards, peak speeds, jitter measurements, bufferbloat analysis, and experience quality scores for gaming, streaming, and video calls.
