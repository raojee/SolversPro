Frequently Asked Questions

How does this Internet Speed, Jitter & Bufferbloat Test work?
Our speed test is powered by Cloudflare's official open-source measurement engine. As soon as you open the page, it performs parallel HTTPS GET and POST requests against Cloudflare's global edge network across 330+ cities to measure your download speed, bandwidth upload speed, unloaded ping, jitter, and loaded bufferbloat.
What is an internet jitter test and what is a good jitter score?
An internet jitter test measures the millisecond variation in packet arrival times across consecutive ping probes. A good jitter test score is under 5 ms for competitive online gaming and VoIP video calls, while jitter above 15–20 ms can cause voice stutter and rubber-banding.
What is a bufferbloat test (fibre & WiFi) and why does loaded latency matter?
A bufferbloat test compares your baseline unloaded ping against your loaded latency while your fiber or WiFi connection is saturated with downloads and uploads. If loaded ping spikes more than 25 ms above unloaded ping, your router queues are bloated and will cause lag whenever multiple devices stream or download.
How does the bandwidth upload test measure my real upload speed?
Our bandwidth upload test streams progressively larger HTTPS POST payloads (from 100 KB up to 25 MB) directly to the nearest Cloudflare edge node to measure sustained upstream throughput and peak upload bandwidth.
Why does my speed test result differ from my ISP plan?
ISPs advertise theoretical maximum "burst" speeds under optimal laboratory conditions. Real-world speeds can be affected by Wi-Fi interference, distance from your router, network congestion during peak hours, outdated router hardware, background app updates, or ISP traffic throttling.
