70+ Free Online Tools in One Place: Discover SolversPro's Comprehensive Toolkit

Today 4 min read

In a digital landscape cluttered with single-purpose websites, ad-heavy interfaces, and tools locked behind paywalls, finding reliable utilities shouldn't feel like a treasure hunt. SolversPro's Online Tools collection changes that equation, offering over 70 free, browser-based tools organised into logical categories, all accessible without signups or subscriptions.

What Is SolversPro?

Located at solverspro.com/tools and our various category pages, this resource hub brings together utilities that developers, designers, marketers, engineers, students, and everyday users need regularly. Rather than bookmarking dozens of separate websites, you get a single destination covering everything from PDF manipulation to financial calculators, password generation to IP lookups.

The collection spans ten major categories, each packed with practical tools that do exactly what they promise—quickly, securely, and entirely in your browser.

Developer Tools: Code and Debug

Developers will find daily-use utilities in this category, built to handle common formatting and conversion tasks.

  • JSON Formatter & XML Tools: Check syntax and format data for readability.
  • Hash Generator: Generate MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, and more hashes instantly.
  • Regex Tester: Test and debug regular expressions in real-time.
  • Base64 Converter: Encode and decode Base64 strings.
  • JWT Decoder: Decode JSON Web Tokens to inspect their payload.
  • Color Converter: Translate colors between HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK.
  • Number Base Converter: Convert between binary, octal, decimal, and hexadecimal.
  • Timestamp Converter: Convert Unix timestamps to human-readable dates and vice versa.

PDF Tools: Manipulate and Convert

Working with PDFs is unavoidable, and these tools make common operations effortless—all processed locally on your device for maximum privacy.

  • Merge & Split PDFs: Combine multiple documents or extract specific pages.
  • Protect & Remove Password: Secure your documents or remove restrictions from files you own.
  • Flatten & Preflight: Prepare documents for printing and analyze their contents.
  • Compose: Build new PDFs from scratch.
  • Conversions: Seamlessly convert Images to PDF, or extract content with PDF to Images, PDF to Text, and PDF to HTML.

Finance & Math: Calculate with Confidence

Whether you're planning investments or solving complex equations, our calculators provide professional-grade accuracy.

  • Finance: Compound Interest, Simple Interest, ROI, Mortgage, and Cash-on-Cash Return calculators.
  • Math: A full-featured Scientific Calculator, Quadratic Solver, Statistics, Geometry, Matrix operations, and Percentage calculators.
  • Unit Converter: Instantly convert between hundreds of units across length, mass, temperature, and more.

Network & Security: Verify and Protect

Essential utilities for sysadmins and security-conscious users.

  • IP Subnet Calculator: Calculate network address, broadcast, and host ranges using CIDR notation.
  • My IP Checker & IP Address Lookup: Find your public IP or look up geolocation, ISP, and ASN data for any address.
  • Password Generator: Create secure, complex passwords with customizable requirements.

Text & Utilities: Generate and Transform

Handy tools for daily tasks and specialized projects.
