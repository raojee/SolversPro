Frequently Asked Questions

How does the SolversPro Internet Speed Test work?
Our internet speed test is powered by Cloudflare's official open-source measurement engine. As soon as you open the page, it performs parallel HTTPS GET and POST requests against Cloudflare's global edge network across 330+ cities to measure your true download speed, upload speed, unloaded ping, jitter, and loaded bufferbloat.
How do I run an accurate WiFi speed test vs. a wired network speed test?
To run a WiFi speed test, open this page in the room where you normally use your device; the test starts automatically and measures wireless throughput and jitter. To test your maximum ISP connection speed, connect your computer directly to your router with a Cat6 Ethernet cable and click the circular re-test button.
Why does my speed test result differ from my ISP plan?
ISPs advertise theoretical maximum "burst" speeds under optimal laboratory conditions. Real-world speeds can be affected by Wi-Fi interference, distance from your router, network congestion during peak hours, outdated router hardware, background app updates, or ISP traffic throttling.
What is bufferbloat (loaded latency) and why does it matter?
Bufferbloat is high latency that occurs when your router buffers too many packets during heavy downloads or uploads. If your loaded latency is significantly higher than your unloaded ping, your connection will experience lag and stuttering whenever multiple devices are active on your network.
How can I improve my internet connection speed?
To maximize your connection speed: (1) Use an Ethernet cable instead of Wi-Fi whenever possible; (2) Place your Wi-Fi router in a central, elevated location away from thick walls and metal objects; (3) Restart your modem and router periodically; (4) Close bandwidth-heavy background downloads or cloud syncing apps; (5) Upgrade your router firmware.
