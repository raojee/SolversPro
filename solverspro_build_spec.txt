                              LOGO CONCEPT
================================================================================

CONCEPT: "The Precision Solver"

Visual: An abstract geometric mark combining:
  • A magnifying glass (represents "finding solutions")
  • Merged with a gear/cog (represents "tools & mechanics")
  • The negative space forms an "S" (for Solvers)

Design:
  ┌─────────┐
  │    ●    │  ← Small circle (lens focal point)
  │   /S\   │  ← "S" shape formed by gear teeth + lens handle
  │  ═════  │  ← Base line (stability)
  └─────────┘

Color Usage:
  • Primary mark: #8CC0EB (sky blue) on #FFF9D2 (cream) background
  • Dark mode: #BFDDF0 (light blue) on #1a1a2e (dark navy)

Wordmark: "SolversPro" in Inter Bold, tight letter-spacing (-0.02em)
  • "Solvers" in #1a1a2e (dark)
  • "Pro" in #8CC0EB (blue accent)

Favicon: Simplified "S" mark inside a rounded square
